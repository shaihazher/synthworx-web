Privacy

Outcome Prompt is operated by DNS Ventures LLC under the Synthworx brand.

Web rewrites

When you press Rewrite, we send your draft and any prior prompt you choose to revise to DeepSeek through its API. Use text you have permission to share. Your draft stays in the page until you submit it. Our application does not keep the source text. For about ten minutes after a response, our server keeps an encrypted copy of the result so a retry can recover it. Our hosting provider and model provider handle requests under their own policies.

Usage and payments

A first-party browser cookie remembers your free balance and the currently selected pack. We keep request identifiers, a keyed digest for retries, times, token counts, referral channel, copies and voluntary ratings to operate the service and understand repeat use. A daily rotating network identifier limits free use. A pack is associated with opaque Dodo customer and grant identifiers. We do not place advertising trackers on this page.

Dodo Payments handles checkout, billing details and emailed pack keys. A key you enter is checked with Dodo. Our application never receives payment card details. Contact hello@synthworx.com for a data request and include enough information to locate your purchase. Keep your pack key private.

Downloadable plugin

The plugin runs in the host agent. Its instructions use no external tools or Synthworx server. The host’s privacy policy applies to that conversation. We cannot see its contents or measure individual plugin use.

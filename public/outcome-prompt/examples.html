Three ways to rewrite the ask.

Which part of a request describes the result, and which part describes a possible way there?

Choose a supplier before Friday

Original

Build me a 20-column spreadsheet for our three suppliers. We need to pick one by Friday. The order must arrive by May 8 and stay under $800. I need to approve the order.

Outcome prompt
Help me choose between our three suppliers by Friday. Find an option that can deliver by May 8 for under $800, and bring it to me for approval before ordering.

The spreadsheet was one way to compare the suppliers. The dates, budget and approval determine whether the recommendation is usable.

Learn a skill for an exam

Original

Make 25 slides on pivot tables. I need to build one from a sales dataset on my own in tomorrow’s exam.

Outcome prompt
Help me learn to build a pivot table from a sales dataset on my own in time for tomorrow’s exam.

A worked exercise could serve the learning goal. The agent still knows what the learner must be able to do unaided.

Deliver a file someone needs

Original

Write a three-sentence website outage notice for our status page. Readers need to know when the next update will arrive. We promised it at 4 p.m. UTC.

Outcome prompt
Write a three-sentence outage notice we can post on our status page. Tell readers to expect the next update at 4 p.m. UTC.

Here the publishable notice is the deliverable. The rewrite keeps the format and the promised time.

How to cut a long federal resume to two pages

Keep the work that proves you meet the announcement, trim repeated duties, and check the file you will actually upload to USAJOBS.

Mark what earns space before making cuts

Save a copy of your full work history and open the announcement beside a new draft. For each required qualification, mark the strongest place your history demonstrates it. Keep a role from ten years ago if it contains the only example of a required task. Reserve space for contact details, the dates and hours for each included role, and any education or credentials the vacancy asks for.

Turn a duty list into a work example

In this fictional draft, “Prepared reports, maintained trackers, coordinated status meetings, and supported management” takes space while leaving the reader to guess what the applicant contributed. Suppose their actual work was resolving inconsistent procurement records. A tighter bullet reads, “Reconciled purchase requests across three field offices and flagged duplicate orders in the weekly exception report used by the purchasing lead.” Use details from your own work and spell out terms an outside reader might miss.

Choose between repeated bullets

Imagine your last three roles all include preparing a weekly report. If the announcement asks for briefing senior staff, keep the role where you explained an unusual result to a director and answered follow-up questions. Compress the other reporting bullets unless they prove another requirement. Scan the space gained for missing evidence, such as the tool the vacancy specifically names or a required license.

Check the exported file

USAJOBS applies a two-page limit to resumes uploaded or built in a profile. Its standard upload guidance recommends U.S. Letter pages and PDF for stable formatting, with a 5 MB maximum. Open the exported file itself. Check its page count, confirm names and dates remain together, and read the last line of each page. If a vacancy requests a long-form clinical or research CV, follow its separate document instructions.

How to list hours per week when your schedule or role changed

Show a promotion, a switch from part time to full time, or overlapping jobs clearly enough that a federal hiring reviewer can follow the dates.

Put the schedule beside the role

USAJOBS asks for the month and year you started and ended each relevant role and the hours you worked per week. Keep those details beside the employer and title. Include the pay plan, series, and grade for federal positions. If the vacancy gives a more specific instruction, follow it. A reviewer should be able to tell which hours and which dates go with an accomplishment.

Give a promotion its own dates

Consider a fictional applicant at Lake Street Services. Under one employer heading, they list “Operations Coordinator | 03/2024–08/2024 | 20 hours/week” and “Operations Lead | 09/2024–05/2025 | 40 hours/week.” They put the intake-tracking bullet under Coordinator and the new responsibility for assigning a four-person team under Lead. This preserves the actual start date of the supervisory work. If only the schedule changed, use two dated schedule lines under the same title.

Make an average reproducible

For a variable schedule, check time records and the announcement’s instructions. As a simple fictional example, 144 regular hours over eight consecutive weeks averages 18 hours per week. A line such as “18 hours/week on average, 01/2025–02/2025” describes that pattern if those dates match your records. If long gaps or recurring seasonal breaks would make an average misleading, identify the actual periods worked. Keep your supporting notes for reference.

Show overlapping work as it occurred

If a relevant volunteer shift overlapped with a paid job, list each role with its own dates, weekly hours, and work performed. OPM generally prorates part-time experience under its General Schedule policies. It also limits how much qualifying time an agency can credit within a twelve-month period. The agency applies those rules to the duties and time you document. Its contact in the announcement can address an unusual schedule.

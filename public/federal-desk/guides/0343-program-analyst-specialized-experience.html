How to show specialized experience on a 0343 program analyst resume

Turn a qualification from a U.S. federal job announcement into a bullet a reviewer can trace to a specific job, decision, and result.

In this guide

Start with the grade you intend to apply forMatch each action to a piece of workWrite the smallest bullet that proves both actionsRead the bullet back against the vacancy Official sources

Start with the grade you intend to apply for

Open the Qualifications section of the announcement and find the paragraph for your target grade. Copy it to your private notes. Circle the actions it names and underline what each action applies to. Keep words such as “and” and “or” intact. OPM uses series 0343 for management and program analysis, but an analyst who evaluates service delivery may need different experience from one who analyzes a budget. The vacancy tells you which examples to look for.

Match each action to a piece of work

Suppose a fictional announcement asks for experience analyzing processing delays and recommending changes to management. Write one note for each action. For “analyzing delays,” a fictional county analyst can point to case timestamps they compared and the handoff where requests stalled. For “recommending changes,” they can point to a revised routing rule they presented to the operations manager. Both notes belong under the county role where the work happened.

Write the smallest bullet that proves both actions

A worked example using invented facts reads, “Compared intake and review timestamps for 240 permit applications. Found that transfers added a median of four days and recommended a single intake queue to the operations manager, who approved a six-week pilot.” The sample size describes the analysis. The pilot tells the reader what happened to the recommendation. If the pilot had yet to run, claiming faster processing would go beyond the evidence.

Read the bullet back against the vacancy

Can a reader identify what you personally analyzed, which judgment you made, and who used the work? Place the bullet beneath its job title, employer, dates, and hours so the reader can locate it in time. If another required action, such as briefing executives, remains uncovered, look for a second genuine example. When a number is unavailable, name the decision, document, or change you can substantiate.

How to list volunteer work on a federal résumé

Use relevant unpaid work to show how you meet a U.S. federal job requirement. Give the reviewer the dates, hours and work they need to evaluate it.

Choose volunteer duties that match the vacancy

Open the Qualifications section and find the specialized experience for your target grade. Pick the part of your volunteer work that shows you can do a task at that level. For General Schedule qualifications, OPM directs agencies to evaluate unpaid work using the same criteria as comparable paid work. The agency looks at what you actually did and how it fits the vacancy.

Give the role its own dates and hours

If the role is one of your strongest examples, put it with your relevant experience and identify it as volunteer work. Include the organization, your role, start and end month and year, and hours per week. A fictional entry might read “Volunteer Scheduling Coordinator, Harbor Food Pantry | 02/2025–06/2025 | 6 hours/week.” If the schedule varied, use a defensible average for the active period and list separate periods when a break or schedule change would make one average misleading.

Show the work in a bullet

Imagine the vacancy calls for maintaining schedules and resolving coverage gaps. A fictional bullet could read “Maintained the weekly roster for 18 volunteers across two distribution shifts. Reviewed changes with shift leads, reassigned volunteers who had agreed to cover open shifts, and sent the updated roster to the pantry manager.” Replace these details with your own. Describe your decision or output if you have no defensible numbers.

Keep the role distinct from overlapping work

If the volunteer work overlapped a paid job, give each role its own dates and weekly hours. OPM generally prorates part-time experience for General Schedule qualifications, and the agency determines which duties and time qualify. Check the announcement for any additional instructions. Within the two-page résumé limit, keep the volunteer bullet if it proves a required task more clearly than another entry.

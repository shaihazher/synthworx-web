Terms & privacy.

Using the book

DNS Ventures LLC operates Arrival Slip under the Synthworx brand. Use it on forms and destinations you have permission to test. Each receipt records an operator’s observation at the time of the check. Review it before sharing it with a client.

Billing

The free edition holds one filed receipt. A Studio subscription costs US$49 per month for one operator, plus applicable taxes, and renews monthly until cancelled. Cancel renewal in the billing portal. For a delivery problem, duplicate charge or refund request, email hello@synthworx.com with your receipt number. Your applicable consumer rights continue to apply.

Where data goes

Client names, form addresses, excerpts, optional clips, reports and draft slips are stored on this device. The optional Chrome helper reads a highlighted selection when you invoke it. If you include an image, the helper captures the visible tab in memory and attaches the selected crop to the receipt. The workspace shares a license key and device activation with Dodo Payments for access checks. Dodo handles purchases, receipts and billing details. A scoped access cookie remembers Studio in this browser.

We count checkout starts, activations and selected workspace actions by day and acquisition channel. We keep these counts separately from client evidence. Hosting and billing providers process the technical information needed to serve pages and take payments. Support correspondence goes to the Synthworx mailbox. The receipt book’s Local book data control erases your Arrival Slip records from this browser. Email hello@synthworx.com for privacy and account requests.

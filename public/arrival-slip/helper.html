Optional Chrome helper

Clip the evidence.

Send a highlighted excerpt and an optional cropped image from the active tab straight to your open receipt. The web desk works on its own.

  1. Download the Chrome helper ZIP and extract it into a folder you will keep.
  2. This is an unpacked pilot extension. In desktop Chrome, open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, select Load unpacked, and choose the extracted folder containing manifest.json. On a managed browser, follow your administrator’s extension policy.
  3. Open a new check in the receipt book and keep that tab open. Highlight only the confirmation or test entry on the page you are checking, then click Arrival Slip in the Chrome toolbar.
  4. Review the excerpt and optional crop. Choose which side of the receipt it belongs to. You can remove the crop or edit the text before filing.

Chrome grants access to the tab when you open the helper. Your client excerpts and crops stay in this browser.

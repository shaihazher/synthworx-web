Support & billing

Your subscription.

Studio is US$49 per month for one operator. Dodo Payments emails your license key after payment.

Activate or manage access

Open the receipt book and enter your key. Use the Dodo billing portal to manage your payment method, download invoices or cancel renewal. Past receipts remain readable on your device.

Feedback or delivery help

Email hello@synthworx.com. Tell us which check you were doing and what made it hard. For a billing or refund request, include your receipt number. Keep client evidence and license keys out of support email.

Storage and recovery

The book is local to the browser profile where you created it. Studio includes a backup file that you can restore on a different device. Keep a PDF of any receipt you want to share.

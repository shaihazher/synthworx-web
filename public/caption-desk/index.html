Before you deliver.

Filmhub, a service that distributes films, requires English captions for every title. A final viewing checks that the captions match what the audience hears.

Which rules are checked?

Timing checks cover overlapping cues, gaps under 42 ms, and durations outside 0.6–8 seconds. Layout checks flag more than two lines, lines over 43 visible characters, and reading speeds above 25 characters per second. The parser also flags malformed cue blocks.

Filmhub’s caption specifications

What still needs a playback review?

Listen against picture to verify sync, dialogue, speaker changes, lyrics, and sound descriptions. The scan reads the SRT alone. Filmhub makes its own acceptance decision. Caption Desk is independently operated by Synthworx.

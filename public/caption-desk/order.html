Your workbench download.

After a successful payment, Dodo Payments emails your receipt and access to the workbench. Check the email address used at checkout, including its spam folder. You can also reopen your order in the Dodo customer portal linked from your receipt.

Open your workbench

Open the private editor link in your purchase email or portal to work in your browser. To work offline, follow these steps.

  1. Download and extract Caption-Desk-Workbench.zip.
  2. Open Caption Desk.html in a desktop browser.
  3. Choose a UTF-8 SRT, or try the station sample.

If your checkout was interrupted, check for a receipt before trying again. For download help, email hello@synthworx.com with your order reference.

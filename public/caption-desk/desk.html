How a film moves through the desk.

An SRT is a text file with a start time, end time, and words for each caption. A distributor or post house sends the file’s technical findings to the desk.

1. Check the caption file on your device

Choose the SRT and give the film a nickname, such as Film A. Your browser reads the file. When you submit, we receive the findings and a code identifying that version. The film and its dialogue stay with you.

2. Give the editor the fix list

We email a printable report and a spreadsheet-friendly list. Each item points to a caption by time or source line, explains the problem and suggests a fix. The editor changes the file.

3. Recheck the editor’s revision

Send the next version after the edit. We compare the issue counts and identify the exact files checked. Your monthly report shows which films and recurring issues still need attention.
